## Goal
In ~50 s: why Melaka became a world port — the strait and monsoons, the Ming
fleets, the goods, and how control passed from power to power.

## Audience & channel
General Malaysian audience; YouTube (16:9) and Reels/Shorts (9:16).

## Key message
A narrow strait, a turning wind, and goods from three worlds made Melaka a
prize — which is why it changed hands.
