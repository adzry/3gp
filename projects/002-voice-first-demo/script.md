# Script — Voice-first demo

Read these lines as written (small deviations are fine — scenes are anchored
to the **bold** opening words of each line, which should stay intact).
Record in one take if you can, ~2.5 words per second, natural pauses between lines.
Save as `public/projects/002-voice-first-demo/voiceover.wav` (or .mp3/.m4a and
update `voiceover.src` in video.json).

## 1. Hook — TitleCard
VO: This video wasn't timed by hand. It was timed by my voice.

## 2. Record — Footage
VO: I recorded the narration first. Then Whisper, running on my own machine, wrote down every word and exactly when I said it.

## 3. Anchor — KineticText
VO: Each scene is anchored to a phrase in that transcript. When I say the line, its scene begins, right on the word.

## 4. Commands — MetricCard
VO: From a recording to a finished video takes three commands: transcribe, preview, render.

## 5. One clock — Footage
VO: The footage, the graphics and the captions all follow the same clock: the sound of my voice.

## 6. Re-record — KineticText
VO: And if I record a line again, the words move, and every scene moves with them. Nothing is re-timed by hand.

## 7. Sign-off — LogoReveal
VO: Voice first. Then everything else. This is 3gp.
