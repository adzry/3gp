# From 3GP to 3gp

- **Created:** 2026-09-28

- **Format / style:** landscape · vox-editorial (+ vertical · studio variant)
- **Composition ids:** `p001-from-3gp-to-3gp`, `p001-from-3gp-to-3gp-vertical`

## Goal
Prove the 3gp system end to end: a project scaffolded from a recipe, built only
from reusable templates, rendered in two formats and two styles from one
`video.json` — with no bespoke animation code.

## Audience & channel
Me / future collaborators reading the repo README. ~30 s.

## Key message
3gp is named after a tiny-video file format; it now exists to make every
video faster to produce than the last.

## Constraints
No voice-over yet (caption-free, silent). Must render offline.
